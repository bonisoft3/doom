# Doom Arena — UAC Phobos Terminal

The identity is **UAC Industrial Terminal**: cold Martian steel, radioactive green hazards, amber telemetry, and the battle-worn armor of a space marine.

## Direction

- **Industrial Steel & CRT Glow**: The interface is not a clean web page; it is a tactical marine terminal. Backgrounds are deep cold charcoal (`#0D0F11`), bordered by heavy riveted steel plates (`#3B434C`).
- **Amber Digital Readouts**: Classic amber LED readouts for ammo and health counters (`#FF9900`), glowing against dark recessed displays.
- **The Doomguy Face**: The iconic status bar mugshot reflects health tiers, pain flashes, and malicious grins when picking up heavy ordnance.
- **Pure Canvas Viewport**: The 3D world renders in a central framed viewport, bordered by tactical diagnostics, while the killfeed floats non-intrusively in the upper right.
