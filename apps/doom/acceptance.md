# Acceptance

- [ ] Opening the arena lands immediately in the 3D deathmatch with a ready status bar and initial marine stats. {#accept-instant-arena}
- [ ] Ammo, Health, Armor, and Arms display the initial player stats correctly. {#accept-status-hud}
- [ ] Doomguy face shows healthy grin initially, switches to grimace when health drops, and dead state at 0. {#accept-doomguy-face}
- [ ] Registering a kill creates a frag feed entry with killer, weapon icon, and victim. {#accept-frag-feed}
- [ ] Selecting another weapon slot switches active weapon and updates ammo counters. {#accept-weapon-switch}
- [ ] Reaching the frag limit transitions match phase to over and reveals the rematch button. {#accept-match-over}
- [ ] The game boots and operates cleanly offline without network connectivity. {#accept-offline}
