Doom Arena — IR (Architecture & Contracts)

Status: Pinned architecture for real-time 3D deathmatch on Pronto.

1. Topology & Planes

  • Kinetic Fast Plane (35Hz): Flecs ECS in WASM + GGRS client prediction. Unreliable UDP/WebTransport or in-tab Worker MessageChannel.
  • Relational State Plane (Durable): Mecha + ElectricSQL + PGlite. Reactive IVM queries for leaderboards, matches, frags, and accounts.
  • Dual Terminal (Presentation): Babylon.js (Canvas 3D) + Omnishell (DOM HUD & Status Bar).

4. Flows

start-match

Initiates a new arena match session, transitioning status from warmup to active.

record-frag

Records a registered kill event and increments frag counts for killer.

update-player

Updates local marine health, armor, ammo, and active weapon state.

5. Handlers

referee

Match referee evaluating combat damage, frag milestones, and match termination in SES sandbox.

match_control

Iniciar starts a match in warmup and Outra Partida resets one; the engine follows the match row.

enter_arena

Processes lobby form submission and configures player marine parameters in SES sandbox.

6. Invariants & Acceptance Tests

Opening the arena lands immediately in the 3D deathmatch with a ready status bar and initial marine stats.

Ammo, Health, Armor, and Arms display the initial player stats correctly.

Doomguy face shows healthy grin initially, switches to grimace when health drops, and dead state at 0.

Registering a kill creates a frag feed entry with killer, weapon icon, and victim.

Selecting another weapon slot switches active weapon and updates ammo counters.

Reaching the frag limit transitions match phase to over and reveals the rematch button.

The game boots and operates cleanly offline without network connectivity.

7. Architectural Decisions

  • Levels and geometry are expressed in standard Khronos glTF 2.0 (e1m1.gltf) with KHR_physics_rigid_bodies rather than bespoke formats.
  • The 3D canvas is mounted behind the Babylon.js domain boundary (@babylonjs/core + @babylonjs/loaders) following monorepo dependency hygiene.
  • Health, armor, ammo, and the iconic Doomguy face are rendered in real DOM with Omnishell templates and CSS, eliminating canvas text overhead.
  • The 35Hz simulation operates with deterministic integer fixed-point math and zero ambient authority.
  • Escape hatches: none. All state and interactions are modeled strictly through deterministic ECS machines, glTF 2.0 schemas, and pure Jessie sandbox compartments.
